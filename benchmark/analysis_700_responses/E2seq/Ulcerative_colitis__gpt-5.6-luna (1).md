## 1. Overall biological interpretation

The UC mucosal profile is most consistent with an inflamed and structurally remodeled colonic epithelium accompanied by altered transport and metabolic functions and increased immune-cell or immune-response signals.

Three broad contrasts dominate:

1. **Strong induction of innate inflammatory and antimicrobial programs**, including `DUOX2`, `DUOXA2`, `S100A8`, `LCN2`, `PI3`, `CXCL1`, `CXCL2`, `CXCL3`, `MMP3`, and `CHI3L1`.
2. **Suppression of epithelial transport, absorptive, metabolic, and barrier-associated genes**, including `AQP8`, `AQP7`, `SLC51A`, `SLC38A4`, `SLC16A1`, `ABCG2`, `HMGCS2`, `MEP1B`, and `DEFB1`.
3. **A wound-repair, extracellular-matrix, and mucosal-remodeling response**, represented by increased `TNC`, `TGM2`, `TIMP1`, `PDPN`, `PRRX1`, `CDH3`, `SERPINB5`, and `TRIM29`.

The simultaneous increase in inflammatory, remodeling, and immunoglobulin-associated signals and decrease in differentiated epithelial transport genes could represent active UC biology, but could also be partly generated by **replacement of mature epithelial cells by infiltrating leukocytes, stromal cells, or regenerative epithelial populations**.

## 6. Major limitations and alternative explanations

1. **Cellular composition:** Increased neutrophil, lymphocyte, plasma-cell, or stromal content can inflate inflammatory and immune transcripts, while loss of mature colonocytes can reduce transport and metabolic genes. Single-cell, spatial, histological, and deconvolution analyses are needed.
2. **Disease activity and treatment exposure:** Active inflammation, steroid or biologic treatment, recent antibiotics, and disease severity could substantially alter these programs. Clinical metadata and stratified analyses are required.
3. **Technical and annotation redundancy:** The 175 retained rows include duplicated and composite entries, including multiple `CYP2B6`, `NAT8B`, `ETNK1`, `WDR78`, `CTLA4`, and other records. Probe-level quality control and gene-level collapse should be reviewed.
4. **Unusually universal significance:** All 100 unique genes have FDR ≤ 0.01. This may reflect genuine separation, but should prompt inspection of sample clustering, replicate structure, batch variables, variance estimates, normalization, and whether controls and UC samples were processed together.
5. **Association-versus-causation:** The results describe a UC-associated mucosal state. They do not establish that any gene causes UC, drives relapse, predicts treatment response, or is therapeutically effective.

Overall, the most defensible interpretation is a **UC-associated inflammatory and tissue-remodeling state with impaired mature epithelial transport and metabolic signatures**, while the relative contributions of intrinsic epithelial dysfunction and altered cellular composition remain to be experimentally separated.
